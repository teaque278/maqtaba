Who are we?

Maqtaba is a word for library, and that is exactly what we are building: a library that lives in the cloud, so the books are there wherever you are.

Open to everyone

A place for readers of every background to find something worth reading.

Always on

No opening hours and no due dates. Pick up where you left off, on any device.

Built with care

A collection that grows through the people who love it.

FAQs

What is Maqtaba?

Maqtaba is a cloud library: a place to browse and read a growing collection from any device.

Do I need an account?

Browsing is open to everyone. Accounts will be needed for uploading and saving titles.

How do I find a book?

Use the search box in Browse, or tap a category to narrow the list.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The site adapts to phones, tablets and computers.
